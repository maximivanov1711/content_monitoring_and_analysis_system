# Automatic content monitoring and analysis system

## AI Saves $2,000+ Per Month for an Info Business on Content Search and Analysis

## Full Presentation
https://gamma.app/docs/Content-monitoring-and-analysis-system-goh8nv4bq8klxid

## Client
An info-business blogger with a million-plus audience, lots of fan-generated content, and an affiliate program.

## Problem
- We need to find new content in real time that violates the affiliate program rules or negatively affects the blogger's reputation.
- Every day, more than several hundred pieces of content appear, so processing them requires an entire team of employees, which takes a lot of money and time.
- To have one employee do this work instead of an entire team, automation is necessary.

## Solution
I developed a system that daily searches for content by a list of search queries and analyzes it using AI.

## Result
### 💰 Saving 200k Rubles Per Month
The cost of maintaining the system is **$500 per month**, which saves about **200k rubles per month** compared to a team of employees.

### ⏱️ Time Savings
Automatic content search and analysis in real time instead of waiting a long time for results compared to a team of employees.

### 📊 Detailed Analytics
Collection of metrics for each piece of content and its author (number of views, likes, comments, profile description, number of profile followers, etc.).

### 📦 Over 50,000+ Pieces of Content in Total
Over **50,000 pieces of content** related to the client's brand have been found in total.

### 🔍 1,000+ Pieces of Content Per Day
More than **1,000 pieces of content** related to the client's brand are automatically found every day.

### ⚠️ 100+ Pieces of Suspicious Content Per Day
On average, **100+ pieces of content** suspected of violating the partner program rules are identified every day.

## Features
- Content is searched across social networks **TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch, VK, Telegram, and Zen**; search is also possible on any other websites.
- Search can be limited by date, keywords, author, number of views, and other parameters.
- Video content is automatically converted to text for further analysis.
- The Google Sheet with the results has sorting by publication date, filters by social network, and keyword search set up.
- There is an option to run the system manually for analyzing your own uploaded content.
- Notifications about the system's operation are set up through a Telegram bot.

## Applications
In what tasks is this system needed:
- **Trend Watching**: Tracking trends and adapting popular content to your brand.
- **Statistics**: Tracking UGC content statistics about your brand with profile comparison.
- **Responding**: Quick response to content about your brand.
- **Search**: Finding all content about your brand.
